# TULAY domain context

## Beneficiary

A patient using TULAY to access YAKAP-GAMOT information. A beneficiary account may be Pending or Active; registry matching does not replace in-person clinic verification.

## Laboratory referral

A doctor-issued navigation record for a laboratory service that the assigned YAKAP clinic cannot cover or complete. It tells the beneficiary where to seek the service and why the clinic referred them. It is not a diagnosis, an authorization to perform a test, or a live connection to a hospital or laboratory.

## Formal provider

A hospital or laboratory destination outside the TULAY clinic. In the hackathon demo, the destination is represented as fictional text rather than an accredited-provider integration.

## Referral boundary

Doctors may refer active patients assigned to their clinic. Beneficiaries may view their own referrals. The referral does not activate an account, issue a prescription, or claim that the destination accepted or completed the laboratory service.
